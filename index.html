      Methodology & Limitations

      What PhishGuard V2 Does

      PhishGuard V2 performs strict local parsing of URLs, email/SMS message text, and QR code payloads. It evaluates structural anomalies such as excessive subdomain nesting, suspicious userinfo tokens, IP address hostnames, character encoding confusion, and psychological pressure phrasing without visiting or navigating to untrusted targets.

      Limitations of Heuristics

      Heuristic rules generate probabilistic risk indicators, not definitive security verdicts. Brand-new phishing domains without lexical anomalies can bypass rules, while complex legitimate sites might trigger false positives. Absence of triggered heuristics does not prove a target is safe.

      Privacy Commitment

      All parsing runs client-side in your browser. Scan records are stored locally in your browser's localStorage (limited to the last 20 entries with sensitive query tokens redacted). No data is transmitted to external servers unless an optional threat intelligence API proxy is explicitly configured.
